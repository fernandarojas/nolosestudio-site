BazArt for Venezuela

An online catalog for BazArt, an art fundraiser for Venezuela’s earthquake response through NEXOS.

ClientPaola’s Gallery, for NEXOS
Year2026
Works96 works by 22 artists
BuiltOnline catalog

// The site

Every donated piece in one catalog, filtered by artist, type and status, with a page for each work and a simple way to pay that sends the money straight to the organization.

// The event

One day at Ocisly Ceramics, Miami, on Aug 16, 2026. The works filled the shelves and tables, and a QR code opened its page in the catalog.

22Artists
96Works donated
34Pieces sold
$2,695Raised for NEXOS

Organized by Paola’s Gallery · Figures as of Sept 2026
