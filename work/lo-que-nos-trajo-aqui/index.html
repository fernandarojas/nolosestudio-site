Lo que nos trajo aquí

A pocket zine for a six-artist group show at Laundromat Art Space, Miami.

ArtistsAndrea Ballesta, Fernanda Rojas, Isabella Rodríguez, Luca Otamendi, Paola Moreno, Oliver Wallis
VenueLaundromat Art Space, Miami
DatesJul 11 – 13, 2025
CoversSix, one per artist
BindingFolded and cut
Size2.25 × 3.75 in

// The zine

Made for a three-day summer group show at Laundromat Art Space in 2025: six covers, one for each artist, short lines in Spanish, and a fold-out with every artist’s portrait and bio.

// Process

Cut, scored and folded by hand in the studio

// From the show

Each cover carries a piece from the show. The painting with the ribbon curls and the black flying figure hang on the walls and open their own edition of the zine.

Show photos: Andrea Ballesta
